## SECTION A — Financial Analysis

| # | Criteria | Value | Remarks |
|---|----------|-------|---------|
| 1 | Sales Growth | **CAGR > 15%** (last 7–10 yrs) | Growth should be consistent year-on-year. Ignore companies where a sudden one-year spurt confounds the 10-year performance. Very high growth of **>50% is unsustainable**. |
| 2 | Profitability | **NPM > 8%** | Look for sustained operating AND net profit margins over the years. |
| 3 | Tax Payout | **> 30%** | Tax rate should be near the general corporate tax rate unless specific tax incentives apply. |
| 4 | Interest Coverage | **> 3** | Higher is better. |
| 5 | Debt-to-Equity Ratio | **< 0.5** | Prefer very low debt. Zero debt is ideal. |
| 6 | Current Ratio | **> 1.25** | Indicates adequate liquidity. |
| 7 | Cash Flow | **CFO > 0** | Positive operating cash flow is essential. Ideally CFO covers CFI + CFF outflows. |
| 8 | Cumulative PAT vs CFO | **cPAT ≈ cCFO** | Cumulative PAT and cumulative CFO should be similar over the last 10 years. |

## SECTION B — Valuation Analysis

| # | Criteria | Value | Remarks |
|---|----------|-------|---------|
| 9 | Earnings Yield (EY) | **EY > FD rate / G-Sec yield** | Higher EY vs risk-free alternatives is preferable. |
| 10 | PEG Ratio | **< 1** | Lower PEG = better value relative to growth. |
| 11 | Margin of Safety | Adequate discount to intrinsic value | Buy at a discount to estimated intrinsic value. |

*(Also per Ch.10/Ch.13: P/E via "4 Principles for Ideal P/E"; P/B < 1 mainly for financials; P/S < 1.5, sell >3; DY > 0%, >5% very attractive, ignore for fast-growers.)*

## SECTION G — Margin of Safety

**MoS in Purchase Price**
| Parameter | Desired Value | Remarks |
|-----------|---------------|---------|
| Earnings Yield (EY) | **EY > 10-Yr G-Sec yield** | The higher the gap, the better. |

**MoS in Business Model**
| Parameter | Desired Value | Remarks |
|-----------|---------------|---------|
| Self-Sustainable Growth Rate (SSGR) | **SSGR > Achieved Sales Growth** | Higher SSGR vs actual growth, the better. |
| Free Cash Flow (FCF) | **FCF / CFO >> 0** | Higher FCF as a proportion of CFO preferred. |

*SSGR formula (Ch.9, p.60):* **SSGR = NFAT × NPM × (1 − DPR) − Dep**
where NFAT = net fixed asset turnover, NPM = net profit margin, DPR = dividend payout ratio, Dep = depreciation as % of net fixed assets.

## Key Takeaways
1. Consistent long-term growth > short-term spikes.
2. Sustainable profitability > aggressive expansion.
3. Cash flow quality is critical; accounting profits should convert into cash.
4. Avoid highly leveraged businesses.
5. Look for strong competitive advantages and trustworthy management.
6. Buy only with an adequate margin of safety.
7. Avoid businesses facing significant government interference.
8. Credit quality should be strong and improving.
9. Diversification within a company should be logical and related to the core business.
10. Patience is essential; temporary challenges don't necessarily impair long-term returns.

## Author's Closing Notes
- No checklist can ever be completely exhaustive.
- Companies passing most parameters likely have sound fundamentals and reasonable valuations.
- Investors should keep learning and refine the checklist to their understanding.
- Long-term success = buying good businesses at fair prices and staying invested through volatility.
